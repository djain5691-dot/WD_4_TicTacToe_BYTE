Guide

How to Play Tic Tac Toe

1

Two Players (X & O)

The game is played on a 3x3 grid. Player 1 takes X (Cyan) and Player 2 takes O (Rose). Player X always moves first.

2

Take Turns

Players alternate turns placing their symbol into an empty square. Occupied cells cannot be overwritten.

3

Winning Conditions

The first player to align 3 of their marks horizontally, vertically, or diagonally wins the match!

4

Draw Condition

If all 9 squares are filled without any player achieving 3 marks in a row, the round ends in a Draw (Tie).

⌨️

Keyboard Shortcuts

Press 1 to 9 on your keyboard / numpad to quickly mark cells, R to start a new round, and Esc to close this dialog.
